# 💳 Credit Card Weekly Financial & Operations Dashboard

An end-to-end financial reporting dashboard built using **PostgreSQL, Power BI, and DAX** to analyze weekly revenue growth, spending channels, and customer portfolio risks.

---

### 🚀 Key Project Metrics
* **Total Revenue:** **$57M** | **Total Interest:** **$7.98M**
* **Transaction Volume:** **$45.5M** across **667K transactions**
* **WoW Growth:** **+28.8%** revenue increase in Week 53
* **Product Share:** Blue & Silver cards contribute **93%** of all transactions (Blue card alone generates **$47M**)
* **Geographic Driver:** Top 3 states (**TX, NY, CA**) generate **68%** of total revenue
* **Customer Retention:** **57.5%** 30-day card activation rate | **3.19 / 5.0** CSAT

---

### 🛠️ Tech Stack & Workflow
* **SQL (PostgreSQL):** Ingested and modeled 10,000+ transaction and demographic records.
* **DAX & Power BI:** Engineered custom time-intelligence measures (WoW Revenue, Current vs Previous Week) and dynamic customer demographic buckets (`AgeGroup`, `IncomeGroup`).
* **Visuals:** Built multi-page interactive drill-downs across expense categories (Bills: $14M, Fuel: $10M) and payment channels (Swipe: $36M, Chip: $17M).

---

### 💼 Business Impact
* Identified **40–50 age cohort ($25M)** and **Businessmen ($18M)** as the primary revenue drivers to optimize marketing spend.
* Uncovered high dependency on physical swipes (63%), helping leadership prioritize digital onboarding incentives.
